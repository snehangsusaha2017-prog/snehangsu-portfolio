GUIDED LEARNING JOURNEY

Apollo RetailIQ AI — Case Study

Understanding what was built, design philosophy, and data-driven inferences.

Note: Operating on a synthetic dataset modeled on Apollo Pharmacy's profile.
CHAPTER 1

The Challenge & Opportunity

Data existed across POS, CRM, ERP, and Excel sheets, but answering simple operational questions took days of manual effort. Apollo RetailIQ AI unifies 247+ stores across 10 states into a single, instant filter-driven view.

CHAPTER 2

Design Philosophy

  • Data Before Decoration: Every chart answers a specific business question.
  • Interconnected Filtering: Selecting a state (e.g. Maharashtra) recalculates all KPIs instantly.
  • Context at Every Level: Provides trend, comparison, forecast & AI recommendation for every metric.
  • Google Material 3: Clean whitespace and consistent spacing grid.
