IDENTITY

Who am I?

About Me: A developer, designer, and student of systems who loves bridging technical depth with strategic thinking and product intuition.

What Drives Me: Deep curiosity about how complex systems work — whether distributed software, economic markets, or corporate environments. I want to build tools that solve genuine inefficiencies.

"Learn continuously, build deliberately, and leave things better than you found them."
Developer Economist Designer

EXPERIENCE

What I did?

Education: Pursuing a rigorous academic path combining software engineering, data sciences, and business analytics.

  • Mistakes & Learnings: Every failed deployment and missed projection is a stepping stone. True growth comes from reviewing, debugging, and documenting your mistakes.
  • Karma Yoga: Applying selfless action to my work — helping peers, mentoring juniors, contributing without hyper-focusing on immediate rewards.
  • Placecom Effect: Navigating the unique pressure of academic placement committees: managing student interests, corporate outreach, and operations under deadlines.

PHILOSOPHY · HUMOUR

Fun Philosophy

Humor is essential in navigating complex engineering tasks. My lighthearted rules:

  • The 2 AM Rule: If it doesn't work by 2 AM, sleep will magically fix it by 9 AM.
  • Occam's Butterknife: The simplest solution is usually the best — but sometimes you butter toast with whatever tool you have.
  • Infinite Loops: Coffee is the input. Code is the side-effect.
